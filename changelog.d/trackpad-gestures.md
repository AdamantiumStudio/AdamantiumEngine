### Added

- Fingers panning with Alt held orbit the camera about what is under the pointer, as Alt with the right button does -
  a trackpad has no right button to drag with. The camera's action is `OrbitPan`.
- `Adamantium.MacOS`: the phases, magnification, rotation and device deltas of an event, the cursor let go of by the
  mouse (`AssociateMouseAndCursor`), and the characters of the keyboard layout in use (`Carbon.KeyboardLayout`).
