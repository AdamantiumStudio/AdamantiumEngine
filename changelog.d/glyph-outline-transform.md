### Added

- `GlyphWordData.OutlineTransform`: where a laid-out glyph's outline is drawn, as the screen draws it - from the font's
  units to the layout's space, its stretch, its turn in a vertical line and a synthesized italic's slant in it.
- `PdfContentBuilder.StrokesText`: glyphs stroked as well as filled (text rendering mode 2), for a thickened face.
